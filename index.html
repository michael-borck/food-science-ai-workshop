The Core Idea

AI empowers thinking through brainstorming, ideation, iteration, and amplification. Delegation creates dependency; conversation builds capability.

Delegation Mindset

  • "AI, write this for me"
  • "AI, do my analysis"
  • "AI, give me the answer"
  • Creates dependency and shallow understanding

Conversation Mindset

  • "AI, help me think through this"
  • "AI, what patterns do you see?"
  • "AI, challenge my hypothesis"
  • Builds capability and deeper insight
