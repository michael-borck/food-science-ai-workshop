Conversation, Not Delegation

Using AI to Amplify Your Food Science Research

A 2-hour hands-on workshop for postgraduate researchers. Learn to think with AI, not through it. Covers conversational techniques, the RTCF and VET frameworks, data analysis with Gemini, and literature synthesis with Gemini Notebook.

Dr. Michael Borck 7 March 2026 2 hours Gemini + Gemini Notebook

Topics Covered

  • Conversation, Not Delegation mindset
  • RTCF prompting framework (Role, Task, Context, Format)
  • Reverse prompting, multi-perspective analysis, devil's advocate
  • VET framework (Verify, Explain, Test)
  • Hands-on data analysis with Gemini
  • Literature synthesis with Gemini Notebook
